# T.R. Price — **defi4refi**

**Free, open-source protocol engineering for ReFi & public-goods teams.**

Regenerative-finance projects have world-class vision and no engineering
budget. defi4refi is a public-goods development studio: supporters fund the
studio, and the studio designs and ships protocol codebases **free** to
selected teams — community treasuries, staking systems, local currencies,
bonds, governance.

Every delivery is open source, every engagement gets a public writeup and a
hypercert. Audit-ready, not audited — your team deploys and operates the
contracts under your own keys.

## What we build

| Tier | Deliverable | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| T0 | Design sprint — tokenomics, architecture, threat model, roadmap | ~1 week |
| T1 | Protocol-in-a-box — token, staking, bonds, treasury, governance | ~2–4 weeks |
| T2 | dApp frontend — staking/bonds/dashboard with wallet stack | ~1–2 weeks |
| T3 | Adjacent modules — coordination, identity, DePIN, cross-chain | scoped |

Built from contracts already live in production on Base
([rooted-finance](https://github.com/defi4refi/rooted-finance),
[abyayala](https://github.com/defi4refi/abyayala),
[regen-bridge](https://github.com/defi4refi/regen-bridge)).

## How it's funded

Donations go to the studio — donors receive no services (patronage of a
public good, not a service sale). Funding channels: Giveth, Octant, Gitcoin
rounds, direct crypto, and hypercerts on each delivered engagement.
